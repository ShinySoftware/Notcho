The peek

Closed, the notch shows a wing either side of the camera: what's playing, any system readings, and a running timer, a meeting in the next 15 minutes or the weather. It splits them so both sides stay even. With click to open on, add previous, play/pause and next buttons right there. With nothing to show, it disappears into the camera.

File Tray

Drop files on the notch to shelve them. Copy them back out (⌘V in Finder pastes them), AirDrop them, reveal or open them.

System

CPU, GPU and memory as rings, the last 24 seconds of CPU, and network and disk rates. Updated once a second, only while the tab is open.

Keep Awake

Stops the computer sleeping, and the screen going dark, until you stop it or for up to 4 hours. A cup in the peek says it's on.

Tasks

Check one off and it's struck through, then cleared away a day later.

Clipboard

What you copied lately. Click to copy it again, pin the ones you reuse. Concealed passwords are never kept.

Calendar

The rest of today and tomorrow, from every calendar you have. Add an event without leaving the notch.

Mirror

Your camera, flipped the way a mirror is. Step through looks and take a photo straight into the tray.

Voice Notes

One click to record, with a timer and level meter. Play them back or drop them in the tray.

Teleprompter

Your script scrolls right under the lens, so you read while looking at the camera.

Calculator

× and ÷ before + and −, in decimals, so 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3. Recent sums sit beside the keypad.

Your ten busiest processes, sortable by CPU, memory, disk or network. Force quit in two clicks.

One-click shortcuts into macshot: area, screen, scrolling, text and recording. The notch folds away first.

How it works

Nothing polls while nothing changes.

Music and Spotify announce every play, pause and track change. CoreAudio reports volume. DisplayServices reports brightness. The clock ticks once a second only while something on screen counts seconds, and a countdown is a single timer set for its end.

  • Native AppKit. .NET MAUI on the AppKit head, not Mac Catalyst.
  • No icon assets. SF Symbols are drawn once and cached.
  • No keyboard grabs. The notch window can never take focus; typing happens in a quick entry popup.
  • No Dock icon. It's an agent app. Open it again from Finder to get to Settings.

Built on Shiny open source

The notch is a control.
You can have it too.

Shiny Notch is a showcase for Shiny .NET, the MIT-licensed libraries for .NET MAUI, Blazor and ASP.NET Core. The morphing notch, the music bars, open at login and the calendar all come from packages anyone can use.
